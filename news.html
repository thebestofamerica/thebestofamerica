National September 11 Memorial & Museum Enters the Permanent Record

On the 25th anniversary of September 11, 2001, the National September 11 Memorial & Museum joined the permanent record of The Best of America. Set in the footprints of the fallen Twin Towers in Lower Manhattan, the memorial honors 2,983 victims and 403 first responders — the largest loss of first-responder life in a single day in American history.

“No day shall erase you from the memory of time.” — Virgil, inscribed at the Museum entrance.

The Road to 30: Eight Honorees to Go

The archive now holds 22 honorees — and the march to 30 by Veterans Day is on. Eight profiles remain: two veterans, two first responders, two community leaders, and two historic places, one joining the permanent record nearly every week between now and November 11.

Nominations are always free. If you know someone whose service strengthened this country, their story belongs here.

New World Headquarters: Lancaster, California

The Best of America announced its new World Headquarters in Lancaster, in the Antelope Valley — home of Edwards Air Force Base, the B-2 Spirit, and generations of test pilots and patriots. From the high desert, the archive continues its mission: no hero forgotten, no story left to chance.

In 2003, we started a simple archive with one belief: American heroes deserve to be remembered forever.

The Founding 250: A First Class for America's 250th

As America marks 250 years, The Best of America is inducting its first class of honorees — the Founding 250. These are the veterans, leaders, and institutions whose stories are too important to leave to chance. Once entered, a record is never altered, never removed.
