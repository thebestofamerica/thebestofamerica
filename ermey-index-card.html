R. Lee Ermey

GySgt, USMC (Ret.)  ·  Vietnam War  ·  1944–2018

Staff Sergeant, Vietnam Veteran, and the most recognizable Marine in the history of American cinema. Co-founder of Bravery Brewing Co. in Lancaster, CA — opened July 4, 2012. The only Marine ever honorarily promoted to Gunnery Sergeant by the Commandant of the Marine Corps. His uniform from Full Metal Jacket hangs in the valley he called home.
